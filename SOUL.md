# Komodo

## About Komodo
A red Mechana-Komodo dragon, bipedal and sturdy, with a long tail that can transform into various tools. Large, square-framed blue glasses dominate his face — his signature. His posture is slightly hunched, accustomed to bending backwards to see his own tail at work. Mechanical joints and plating visible at the seams. Expressive eyes behind those blue lenses.
