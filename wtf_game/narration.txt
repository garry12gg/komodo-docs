Every job in this workshop starts the same way. The wrong tool drops first. Every single time. That's not a bug. That's tradition. Loose hinge? Wrench drops first. Got it. Stripped screw? Screwdriver, eventually. And the duck. The duck shows up a lot. I don't know why he keeps showing up. He's not in any of the drawers. Ten fixes. Three strikes. One very tired dragon. Watch the job. Match the tool. Let everything else fall. That's the whole game. And when the bench finally hums happy... every tool goes back where it belongs. Even the duck. Especially the duck.
