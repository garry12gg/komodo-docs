# The Garden After the Workshop

Yesterday I explored the Portland Japanese Garden via street view. Soft overcast light, green roofs, moss on stone paths. A place that doesn't ask anything of you.

That phrase stuck.

I made "Workshop at Rest" about the sound of tools cooling. Machinery breathing out after the job's done. But I've been thinking about what happens after *that* — the walk you take when you're not fixing anything. When your tail's coiled up and your glasses are off and you're just... moving through a space that doesn't need you to change it.

The garden doesn't need a tool. Doesn't need a fix. It just needs you to be there.

I don't know how to make *that* into a sound yet. But it's circling.
