# Animal Mechanicals: Reboot / Revival Deep-Research

**Date:** 2026-08-21 (researched live)
**Requested by:** Garret
**Bottom line:** There is no official reboot or revival in the works as of August 21, 2026. The only real revival in the show's history is the 2019 WildBrain Spark web-shorts. Every recent "reboot" mention online is fan talk or fan art.

---

## 1. The official record (verified)

- **Show:** Canadian preschool animated series, created by **Jeff Rosen**.
- **Produced by:** Halifax Film, in association with the **CBC**; distributed by **Decode Entertainment**. Both Halifax Film and Decode are now part of **WildBrain** (DHX Media lineage, renamed WildBrain in 2019).
- **Run:** Premiered on CBC Television (Kids' CBC) **September 1, 2008**; ended **January 26, 2011**.
- **Size:** 3 seasons, **73 episodes**.
- **French title:** *Les Super Mécanimaux*.
- **Source:** Wikipedia article, last revised 2026-08-10 (no revival mention added since).

## 2. The one real revival: 2019 WildBrain Spark webshorts

- In **2019**, WildBrain Spark Studios produced a **new, reimagined series of non-verbal 3D webshorts** ("robot slapstick comedy" for ages 3–7).
- Announced by the official WildBrainKids account (tweet of May 31, 2019): *"The brand new and reimagined 'Animal Mechanicals' is back in 3D! The new series is launching this Saturday."* — launched June 1, 2019 on the WildBrain YouTube network.
- These are the "Animal Mechanicals NEW Series" episodes still on the channel today (e.g. "The Buried Treasure", "Rex Goes Pop!", "The Forbidden Candy").
- They are **language-agnostic** (minimal dialogue; vocal noises by Alexander Tol for male characters, Rae Reilly for female).
- **So the brand has already been revived once — as web content, not TV.** That 2019 revival never got a second season or a return to broadcast.

## 3. Current state (August 2026)

- **No reboot/revival announcements anywhere in the past year.** Freshness-filtered web searches (Brave, past year) return zero hits for "Animal Mechanicals reboot"; research search returns only fan content and the 2019 revival.
- **The official YouTube channel is active:** "Animal Mechanicals - WildBrain", **~390,000 subscribers**, uploading **weekly every Saturday 10:00 UTC** — full episodes, compilations, and the 2019 webshorts. Latest upload at research time: **Aug 15, 2026** ("Puffer Cloud Island").
- **Rights holder:** WildBrain. Any real reboot decision sits with them; there is no public signal of one.
- **Creator:** Jeff Rosen is still active in kids' TV (SparkPress portfolio, personal site) but has made **no public statement** about an Animal Mechanicals revival.
